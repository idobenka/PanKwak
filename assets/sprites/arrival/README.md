# Arrival-smile beat — Concept 4 (Lily-Pad Boat)

**Author:** FrogGame / Asset Designer · **License:** Proprietary (project original)
**The single highest-value art piece** — the game's smile criterion in pixel form.

Per `story.md` §Concept 4 payoff: Pip rows the whole lake and presents whatever survived.
Fern takes a flower — and the last stubborn dragonfly escapes the jar, loops once, and
settles gently on the flower now in Fern's hand. Two frogs, a bug, a held breath, a laugh.
The gift count scaled Fern's welcome, but **this beat lands at any haul, even near-empty.**

- ~~`arrival_smile.svg`~~ — removed (CATS-129 follow-up): the WON beat is text-only now.
- `arrival_smile_empty.svg` — the empty-handed variant (still drawn on the `arrived_empty` overlay).

## Composition
- Sunset rose-gold sky (the dusk/night palette stop the arrival lives at) over indigo water.
- Pip (right of pad) presenting a small bouquet; Fern (left, at the shore) receiving a
  flower, the other hand gentle. Both warm.
- The dragonfly mid-loop, one wing-settling onto Fern's flower — the focal point.
- String lights + a lantern on the far shore (the share-screenshot background).

## Tone self-check (R7/R8/R9 — all three)
- **R7:** this beat is deliberately drawn to work even for the empty-handed case — see
  `arrival_smile_empty.svg`, where Pip has the apologetic grin and Fern is the *touched*
  pose, and a single dragonfly still lands on her flower. The point was never the perfect
  gift; it was that Pip brought *something* across the lake. Warm anticlimax, never loss.
  (The WON variant illustration was removed — the won overlay is text-only.)
- **R8/R9:** no fish, no menace, no attack imagery anywhere in the arrival scene.
